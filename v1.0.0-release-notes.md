# Fantom's Preview Plus v1.0.0

Everything that's different from the original Fantom's Preview (v2.5.0).

Remove or disable the original Fantom's Preview before installing, they conflict. Needs Lovely. The settings menu needs Steamodded.

## Features
- Score calculates instantly and automatically with no 5-second delay, and the Calculate button is now an Autocalculate On/Off toggle
- The preview automatically updates as soon as a card is selected/unselected or a joker/consumable is reordered, used, or sold.
- New Mod Settings now in the Mods menu or accessible from a gear button next to the Autocalculate toggle
- New Pace colors: Red when you're behind pace (score < chips left / hands left), Orange when "Mr. Bones" would save you from a game over.
- Range animation: when the range comes from luck ("Misprint", Lucky Cards, "Space Joker", "Bloodstone", Lucky Cards with "Bull"/"Bootstraps", "The Hook"), the low number sweeps through every score you could get
- "Raised Fist" with face-down held cards gets its own sweep, labeled with the possible rank, like (9), (10-K), (D) for debuffed, (S) for Stone
- "(NN%)" chance to beat the blind next to the range when it's between 0% and 100%.
- Hover tooltips explaining hidden scores, ranges, the %, and the "Mr. Bones" color
- Modded jokers the preview doesn't know yet show a "?" with a tooltip naming the joker, instead of a wrong number
- Ranges use short M/B numbers (K too when the % is showing) so the left panel doesn't stretch.
- Non-Ranged numbers now follow Balatro's built in e system, starting at e11 instead of e7.

## Bugfixes
- Face-down held cards leaked info: Steel, "Baron", "Shoot the Moon", "Raised Fist", "Blackboard", "Reserved Parking" and "Mime" counted them,
  so the preview could give away what a face down card was. They're now left out and all face down cards are shown as a best/worst case range.
- Plasma Deck + The Hook had event queue bugs, hanging the game for 10-30 seconds after any Play/Discard.
- "The Hook" simulation based preview usually did not work correctly and was overhauled/redone from scratch.
- "The Hook" discards were never factored into any scoring logic, leading to both overcounts and undercounts

## Corrected scores that were too high
- Debuffed jokers still incorrectly gave their Foil/Holo/Polychrome bonus
- "Vampire" and "Midas Mask": a Bonus Card or Stone Card did not properly lose their chip enhancement (but interestingly, cards dealing with Mult did not have this bug)
- "Odd Todd" and "Even Steven" could still trigger from a Stone Card's hidden rank
- "The Hook": counted 0 and 1 card discards (it always discards 2 or rest of player's held cards), and scaling jokers like "Ride the Bus"
  did not reset to its original state (it kept growing) between each calculated combination, so the max was too high.
- "The Hook" + "Ramen": Discards by the boss were not counted in scoring
- "The Hook" + "Green Joker": the Mult it loses from the boss discarding wasn't taken off

## Corrected scores that were too low
- "Bull"/"Bootstraps" + "Mail-In Rebate" + "The Hook": No discard jokers had their effects trigger by the boss, so "Mail-In Rebate" was ignored (this was
  my favorite scoring gap I solved out since it combined so many different scoring issues together).
- "The Hook" + "Hit the Road", "Castle" or "Yorick": Cards discarded by the boss didn't count toward these jokers before calculation.
- "Bootstraps" (but interestingly not "Bull") gave negative Mult when you were in debt.
- "Bull"/"Bootstraps" + "Matador" + "The Ox"/"The Arm": The sim didn't pay out money from Matador before scoring
- "Ride the Bus": Debuffed face cards were incorrectly being treated as resetting the mult to 0.
- "Photograph": Debuffed face cards would still be counted as first face card, which could block a non-debuffed face card from triggering properly.
- "DNA" + "Hologram": "Hologram" didn't grow from any "DNA" copies before scoring.

## All Hover Tooltips
Hover over the score to see these. If multiple tooltips could apply, only the highest one shows.
| When | Tooltip |
|---|---|
| Score shows "??????" | Score is hidden when a selected card or any joker is face down |
| "?" after the score, one unknown modded joker | The Joker "(name)" is not programmed into Fantoms Preview, so this number may be inaccurate |
| "?" after the score, more than one unknown modded joker | Multiple unknown jokers are detected and not programmed into Fantoms Preview, so this number may be inaccurate |
| Range from face down held cards | A range is being shown because a face down card could increase/decrease the score |
| "(NN%)" is showing | This is the probability that the score value will be enough to pass the blind |
| Score is orange ("Mr. Bones") | This score is on-track such that you'll be saved by Mr. Bones |
| Range from "The Hook" | Certain cards being discarded by "The Hook" could impact your score |
| Range from money ("Bull"/"Bootstraps") | Money might increase, which could impact your score |
| Range from mixed luck sources (animation or % turned on) | Multiple mixed probability sources force the preview to disable the animation/percentage -- only the lowest and highest score can be reasonably deduced |
| Range from "Misprint" | Misprint's Mult is random and produces a range of potential values |
| Any other luck-based range | The probability of certain effects triggering could impact your score |
